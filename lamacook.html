LamaCook: cooking Mahala on a rainy day.

A cozy cooking game for iPhone and iPad. Lama wants something warm, so you turn on the stove, pour, sprinkle and stir until the Mahala is ready. I designed, drew and built every part of it myself.

Where it started

I first made LamaCook for Apple's WWDC24 Swift Student Challenge, as a small Swift Playgrounds app. The idea came from a simple feeling: a rainy day in Saudi Arabia, and someone at home making Mahala, a warm dish of dates, whole-wheat flour and black pepper. In Saudi Arabia it's a winter favourite, because it keeps you warm.

Two years later I came back to it and rebuilt it as a full Xcode project, version 2.0. The recipe now follows real steps in order, the pot changes as you cook, stirring swirls the ingredients, and the whole interface is drawn in the same hand-painted style as the game.

Character

Meet Lama

Lama is the heart of the game, so I drew her first. I wanted her to feel friendly and a little shy, with a look kids remember at a glance.

  • Black hair with a warm red light on it, her signature look
  • An oversized lavender jacket with soft blue shading
  • A pink school backpack, ready for the day
  • Bright yellow sneakers to lift the whole palette

Every line keeps a slightly wobbly, hand-drawn edge. That became the rule for the rest of the game.

Interface

Buttons drawn by hand

Instead of standard iOS buttons, I painted the interface like the rest of the art: a honey-coloured plank with a cream edge, and two dates tucked on the corners like decoration.

In version 2.0 the home, replay and sound buttons, the step card and the timer all reuse these two drawings, so nothing on screen looks out of place.

Process

From drawing to game

  1. 1

    Sketch the idea

    One scene with everything you need for Mahala, to see the colours and shapes together.

  2. 2

    Separate the pieces

    Redraw each ingredient, the pot and the buttons on their own layers in Procreate, with transparent backgrounds.

  3. 3

    Bring them to life

    Place them in SpriteKit, so you can drag, tip, pour and stir them with your finger.

The game

Six steps to a warm Mahala

Turn on the stove, pour the hot water, add the dates, the wheat flour and the black pepper, then stir until it's ready. Follow the order to earn three stars.

Built with Swift, SwiftUI and SpriteKit, for iPhone and iPad, in Arabic and English.
